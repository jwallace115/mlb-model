### Item 2 (S33) — realism report

RETURNED (1,312 games x 2,000 sims, league-average teams vs actual 2022-23):
| Band | Sim | Actual | Diff | Bar | HELD? |
|------|-----|--------|------|-----|-------|
| Goals/game ±3% | 6.287 | 6.359 | -1.1% | ±3% | HELD |
| Tied after reg ±2.0pp | 0.191 | 0.230 | -3.9pp | ±2.0pp | NOT HELD |
| SO share ±1.5pp | 0.068 | 0.072 | -0.4pp | ±1.5pp | HELD |
| PP opps/team-game ±5% | 3.770 | 3.835 | -1.7% | ±5% | HELD |
| PP goals/team-game ±8% | 0.706 | 0.640 | +10.3% | ±8% | NOT HELD |
| EN goals/game ±15% | 0.320 | 0.334 | -4.2% | ±15% | HELD |
| Reg by 1 share ±2.0pp | 0.307 | 0.228 | +7.9pp | ±2.0pp | NOT HELD |
| Home win % ±2.0pp | 0.543 | 0.524 | +2.0pp | ±2.0pp | NOT HELD |
| Goal dist max diff ≤0.015 | 0.024 | — | — | ≤0.015 | NOT HELD |

MEANS: The engine produces the right total goals (-1.1%) and the right PP opportunity rate,
SO share, and EN goals. But the distribution is wrong:
- Too few ties after regulation (19.1% vs 23.0%) → not enough variability
- Too many 1-goal regulation wins (30.7% vs 22.8%) → games ending too decisively
- PP goals too high (0.706 vs 0.640 per team) → PP conversion overestimated
- Home effect too strong (54.3% vs 52.4%)
- Goal distribution peaked: too many low totals (k=3: 10.9% vs 9.8%) and too few at k=7

The variance is too low: the engine converges to the mean too quickly. This is a known issue
with second-by-second constant-rate Poisson models — real hockey has more clustering (power plays
create bursts, momentum shifts). The calibration in S-WO4b can address the PP conversion and
home effect; the variance issue may need a variance inflation or overdispersion parameter.
