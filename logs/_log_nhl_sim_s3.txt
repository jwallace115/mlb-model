### Reproducibility check
Rebuilt all 15 event tables from committed generators. sha256 of every shots/penalties/state_time
parquet matches S-WO2 tables EXACTLY (verified per season per table).

### Item 1 (S10) — team ratings

RETURNED: 12,850 team-game rows across 6,425 games (5 seasons). Point-in-time ratings using
ONLY games strictly before each game date. 5v5 attempts FOR/AGAINST per 60, 5v5 xG per attempt.
Stabilised with split-half K on fit seasons (2021-22 + 2022-23).

Split-half reliabilities (fit seasons, 40 games per half):
- 5v5 attempt share: r=0.932, K=6.0 (pre-registered >0.60: **HELD**)
- 5v5 xG per attempt FOR: r=0.798, K=20.7 (pre-registered < att share r: **HELD**)
- PP xG per attempt: r=0.211 (pre-registered <0.40: **HELD**)

MEANS: Team-level 5v5 attempt generation is highly reliable (r=0.93) and needs minimal
regression (K=6). xG per attempt is less reliable (r=0.80) — more regression needed.
PP xG is noisy (r=0.21) — heavy regression, consistent with the known PP randomness.

Season carry-over: not yet implemented (first game of each season starts at league mean).

### Item 2 (S11) — goalie ratings + league finishing term

RETURNED: 12,850 goalie-game rows. GSAx per attempt faced, point-in-time, shrunk.

- GSAx/attempt split-half r: 0.207, K=229.3 (pre-registered <0.30: **HELD**)

MEANS: Goaltending is extremely noisy — even a full season of starts gives an r of only 0.21.
K=229 means massive regression to the mean. This is consistent with the known difficulty of
isolating goalie skill from team defense and shot quality.

League finishing term by month: 2025-26 runs 0.91-0.97 (below 1.0), confirming the 6.8% xG
overprediction for that season.

Starter = goalie on first shot against (not yet validated against box score).

### Item 3 (S12) — ratings-only sanity check

RETURNED: Simplified implied goals from 5v5 team ratings only.
- corr(implied goal diff, actual goal diff): 0.287 (fit), 0.225 (validate)
- Mean implied total: -14.6% (fit), -11.8% (validate) vs actual — **NOT HELD** (+/-3%)
- Pinnacle comparison: **NOT MEASURABLE** (event_id/game_id format mismatch)
- NULL CONTROL: shuffled within date, corr = 0.054 / 0.037 — **HELD** (near 0)

MEANS: The ratings carry real predictive signal (positive correlation, null control confirms).
But the simplified formula badly underestimates total goals because it omits PP/PK contributions
(~1 goal per team per game), the goalie factor, and the league finishing term. The Pinnacle
comparison needs a team+date join (the historical lines use API event_id hashes, not the NHL's
own game_id format). Both are S-WO4 tasks.

### Item 1 (S13) — ratings rebuilt point-in-time

RETURNED: 12,850 team-game rows, 12,850 goalie rows across 6,425 games (5 seasons).
Per-season accumulation with league mean strictly before D in that season.
Carry-over w measured on 2021->2022: att_for=0.784, att_against=0.805.

Split-half reliabilities (per-season halves, fit seasons):
- 5v5 att share: r=0.907, K=8.4
- 5v5 xG/att FOR: r=0.686, K=37.5
- Goalie GSAx/att: r=0.188, K=258.5 (pre-registered <0.30: **HELD**)

NULL CONTROLS:
- Holdout leakage: 2022-23 ratings identical with/without 2024-25+2025-26 (max diff < 1e-10).
  PASS. n=2,624 team-game rows checked.
- Season reset: first game of each season has n_prior_games=0. PASS for all 5 seasons.
- Both tests FAIL on e17ace021 (the old code): old code uses all-season league mean and has
  no season boundary. Verified by checking source code: league_means computed from entire
  2021-26 table, no season variable in the accumulation.

MEANS: The ratings are now clean: no future data, no holdout leakage, per-season reset with
measured carry-over. The reliabilities are slightly lower than S10's (r=0.907 vs 0.932 for att
share) because the per-season split-half has fewer games per half.

### Item 2 (S14) — finishing term label fix

RETURNED: Goals/xG AND xG/goals by month for all 5 seasons. 2025-26 runs goals/xG=0.91-0.97.

MEANS: S9's "Goals/xG 2025=1.068" was xG/goals (inverted). In 2025-26, goals came in ~6.8%
BELOW xG — the model overpredicts. Corrected in the S14 decision entry.

### Item 3 (S15) — full sanity check with Pinnacle

RETURNED: Pinnacle joined by (ET date, home, away) from odds history. Match count: 1,156
(2022-23), 1,138 (2023-24). Full implied goals = 5v5 + PP + goalie + EN.

Pre-registered bars (S12, written before looking):
- corr(implied gd, Pinnacle logit) > 0.60 in 2023-24: **0.810 — HELD**
- corr(implied total, Pinnacle total) > 0.30 in 2023-24: **0.433 — HELD**
- mean implied total +/-3% in 2023-24: **-9.3% — NOT HELD**
- null: shuffled ~0: **0.011 — HELD**

Actual outcomes first (per A1.1):
- corr(implied gd, actual gd): 0.308 (fit), 0.263 (validate)

MEANS: The ratings track Pinnacle strongly (r=0.81) and actual outcomes moderately (r=0.26).
The mean total is 9% low because the simplified PP/PK formula uses league-average PP time
instead of per-team time, and the goalie factor scaling (30x GSAx/att) is rough. The engine
(S-WO4) will use the full simulation, not this formula. The two Pinnacle correlation bars
clear their floors; the absolute calibration bar does not.
