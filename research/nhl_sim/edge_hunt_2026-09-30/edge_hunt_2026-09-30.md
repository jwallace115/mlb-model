# NHL edge hunt — the engine vs the market's move, and the price layer (Cowork, 2026-09-30)

Every test below was pre-registered before its numbers were computed (sha256 given). Scripts are in this folder.

## Test 1 — PREREG_CLV: does the engine predict Pinnacle's line move? FAILED
- **Set-up:**
  - early = Pinnacle ~25 h before puck (median), close = 1.3 h;
  - clean set = both starters are their team's most-used starter before D, and neither team played D-1.
- **Result:**

| season | n | slope of (close − early) on (engine − early) | 90% CI | moved toward the engine (gap ≥ 4 pts) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2022-23 | 290 | −0.027 | [−0.055, +0.001] | 43% |
| 2023-24 (validate) | 289 | −0.004 | [−0.032, +0.024] | 53% |

- On all games (not the test set), 2022-23 is significantly *negative*: Pinnacle moves away from the engine.
- Betting the engine side at the early median price: ROI −4.8% (2023-24), mean CLV −1.6%.
- **Meaning:** the engine's team-strength view holds nothing Pinnacle doesn't already have a day early. With the
  earlier log-loss and A1/A2 results, that makes three independent failures of the engine *as an opinion*.
- Mechanical fix disclosed: 2 games with a missing Pinnacle price produced NaN on the first run. They were dropped
  and the run repeated; nothing else changed.

## Test 2 — PREREG_EV (sha d1957cb6): soft-book price vs Pinnacle fair at the same moment, 2022-24
- **Rule:** best of 8 books, EV ≥ 2% vs Pinnacle's multiplicative de-vig; moneyline, total and puck line.
- **H1 (CLOSE snapshot) FAILED:**
  - 131 bets, ROI −16.6% (SE 10.8%).
  - The bigger the "EV", the worse the result: EV > 5% went 10 bets at −80%.
  - At close, a soft book that is out of line with Pinnacle has usually *already moved on news*. Pinnacle is the
    stale one there.
- **EARLY snapshot (a pre-declared secondary report):**
  - 157 bets, ROI +2.5% (SE 10%).
  - Mean CLV vs Pinnacle's close **+1.28% (SE 0.38%)**: +1.23% in 2022-23 and +1.39% in 2023-24.
- A save-to-parquet crash ended the first run after CLOSE had printed. The fix was to cast one column to string;
  CLOSE was identical on the rerun.

## Test 3 — PREREG_EV2 (sha 7923c858): confirm the early rule on 2024-25 + 2025-26, one run. HELD
- Rule unchanged. The engine is not involved, so the S-WO5 engine holdout is untouched.
- **H2:** mean CLV **+2.70% (SE 0.76%), 90% lower bound +1.44%.**
  - 2024-25: +2.5%;
  - 2025-26: +2.9%.
- ROI −3.2% (SE 15%, 55 bets). That is noise at this sample size; CLV is the lower-variance measure.
- **Caveat — volume:** only ~27 qualifying bets a season at one early snapshot per game. In live use, polling many
  times a day would see more of these gaps; how many more is unknown until forward logging measures it.
- **Caveat — execution:** a snapshot price may be stale by the snapshot gap, and soft books limit winners.

**This is the first NHL lead in four phases that kept its sign on data it wasn't found on.** It is a price edge, not
a model edge.

## Test 4 — PREREG_ALT (sha 17993818): the engine as a pricing layer
Pinnacle's fair price exists only at Pinnacle's own point, but 17% of soft-book total quotes are at another point.
The engine, conditioned on Pinnacle's moneyline and total (two knobs: strength and pace), supplies the full
final-total distribution. That lets it price 6.0 / 5.5 / 6.5 quotes that Pinnacle doesn't hang.
- **Grid:** 9 strength × 7 pace cells × 20,000 sims (market_grid_v1.npz, 14 min on 2 cores).
  - At the centre cell, P(total=6) is 0.106 (actual 2022-24: 0.110 / 0.117).
- **Engine-structure check — PASSED, and informative.** Conditioned only on Pinnacle's moneyline and total, the
  engine's puck-line probability matches Pinnacle's own puck line:
  - mean |diff| 0.7 pts;
  - log-loss 0.6605 vs Pinnacle 0.6606, n = 2,283.
  - **The engine's game STRUCTURE is Pinnacle-grade.** It can turn two sharp numbers into sharp prices for markets
    Pinnacle doesn't post.
- **H3 FAILED:**
  - EARLY: 35 bets, CLV +0.90% (SE 0.83%), 90% lower bound −0.46%. ROI +20% (SE 15%) is noise.
  - CLOSE: 17 bets, ROI −29%.
  - Volume is tiny: only 1,620 off-point quotes at the early snapshot in two seasons.
  - Not re-tuned. There is no confirmation run, per the pre-registration.

## What this means for the layer
1. The engine is not an opinion edge on NHL game lines. That is settled by four tests.
2. The engine IS a sharp pricing machine for game structure. Given Pinnacle's two numbers, it prices derivatives at
   Pinnacle quality. Use: fair prices for markets Pinnacle doesn't hang (team totals, periods, 3-way, alt lines) and
   for Hard Rock's off-market lines.
3. The one confirmed lead is the early price: a soft-book price ≥ 2% better than Pinnacle's fair a day before puck.
   - It held +2.7% CLV out of sample, on other books.
   - Hard Rock (the only book Jeff can bet) is not in any history pull. So the live object (Hard Rock) is NOT yet
     proven identical to the tested object (8 other books). CHECK 3 is open until forward data exists.
4. Next is NHL-L1: an hourly Hard Rock + Pinnacle tape from opening night, a shadow log of every EV ≥ 2% flag and
   every engine-priced derivative, and nightly CLV grading.
