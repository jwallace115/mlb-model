## RETRACTION (LM-1b, 2026-10-11)

**LM-1's C5 and C2 CLV figures are withdrawn.** They compared a de-vigged paid price (HR) with
a de-vigged close (Pinnacle) — both de-vigged, making the comparison a fair-vs-fair drift
measure, not CLV. True CLV is: Pinnacle de-vigged close (fair value) minus the RAW implied
probability of the price actually paid at HR (which includes vig). On this honest metric, every
cell shows negative CLV because HR's vig (~2.3 pp per side) dominates any directional edge.

The corrected table is in `LM_1b_CORRECTED_2026-10-11.md`. Key corrections:

1. **C5** (LM-1 called it "the strongest cell" with +0.91 pp NFL, +1.01 pp NCAAF): on the
   paid-price scale, C5-registered is **−0.75 pp NFL** (valid N=18) and **−1.28 pp NCAAF**
   (valid N=3). The directional shade IS real (drift_pp = +1.59 NFL, +1.01 NCAAF) — HR's
   fair number moves toward Pinnacle's close — but the shade does not overcome the vig.
2. **C2** (LM-1 called the negative CLV a "measurement artifact"): it is negative on BOTH
   scales. Paid-price CLV = −1.91 pp (NFL, valid N=14). The "artifact" explanation was
   correct for the cross-point rows but irrelevant: same-point paid-price CLV is also negative.
3. **NCAAF coverage**: the admission HALT at 31.8% HR coverage should have fired. LM-1 changed
   it to a warning and proceeded. This did not affect the corrected results (NCAAF C5 has only
   3 valid rows) but the override was not authorized.

**What LM-1 got right:** the fire-rate counts, the tape construction, the NCAAF data-gap

**What LM-1 got wrong:** the CLV scale (the central metric), the C5 verdict ("prior
underestimated" — it didn't; C5 fails on the honest metric), and the C2 "not a bug" narrative
(correct diagnosis, wrong conclusion — same-point CLV is also negative at the paid price).
