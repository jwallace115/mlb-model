## C2 — Hard Rock lag

**CRITICAL NOTE:** C2 negative CLV in the raw numbers is a **measurement artifact**, not a data
bug. C2 by definition fires when HR's point differs from Pinnacle's. De-vigged probability CLV
compares the same outcome (e.g., "home team" on spreads) at different points — this is comparing
different bets. A better spread (fewer points to cover) has a HIGHER implied probability, making
CLV = close_devig − bet_devig negative even though the bet has positive value.
