# Exhaustive game-line scan + structural bets (Cowork, 2026-09-29)

Jeff: "the lines have to tell us something — don't stop until you find one that works."

## 1. Pattern scan (sides and totals), 1999-2025 REG, 6,967 games
Splits fixed before running: discovery 1999-2014 (4,072), validation 2015-19 (1,280), holdout 2020-25 (1,615,
not opened for this scan). 106 single conditions — spread/total buckets and key numbers, juice asymmetry, moneyline
vs spread disagreement, division, roof, surface, temperature, weekday, kickoff hour, week, rest, byes, time-zone
travel, each team's last result / last cover / cover rate to date / streaks / revenge, referee prior-season rates
(all point-in-time) — plus line x context and context x form pairs; outcomes home cover, favourite cover, over.
**2,512 tests with n >= 150. Benjamini-Hochberg at 10% FDR: 0 pass. p < 0.01: 15 observed vs ~25 expected by
chance.** Nothing validates. The closing NFL spread and total leave no pattern a public-data scan can find.
(Wind was left out on purpose earlier; nothing here depends on it.)

## 2. Structural bets at real prices (a published hypothesis, so 2015-25 is out-of-sample for it)
Wong teaser (Stanford Wong, 2001): tease underdogs of +1.5 to +2.5 up through 3 and 7 by 6 points.
Leg win rate, total < 49: 1999-2014 73.6% (n=363), 2015-19 78.5% (144), 2020-25 78.2% (234). All totals:
72.7 / 77.4 / 77.0. Favourites -7.5 to -8.5 teased down: 74.0 / 74.4 / 71.3 (no edge).
Break-even per leg: 2-team 6-pt at -120 73.9%, -130 75.2%, -135 75.8%; 3-team at +140 (Hard Rock's own example)
74.7%, +160 72.7%. 2015-25 combined ~78.3% (n=378, se ~2.1pp) -> about +3pp over a -130 break-even, ~1.5 se.
Hard Rock offers 6/7/8/10-point football teasers; its 2-team 6-point price is NOT yet known — Jeff to check in-app.
Volume: ~2 qualifying dogs a week. Moneyline by price (real closing ML 2006+): +400 and longer lose 12-20% in every
era (favourite-longshot bias); -400 and shorter roughly break even.
