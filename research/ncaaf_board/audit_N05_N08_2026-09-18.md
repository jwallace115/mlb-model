# Cowork audit of work order #2 (N05–N08) — 2026-09-18

Independent verification from the Cowork bridge, per the division of labour in `CLAUDE.md`:
reading the artifacts rather than the report, and recomputing the headline numbers from the
committed parquets rather than re-reading `joint_correlation_oos_2025.md`.

**Verdict in one line: the 21+ result is real and robust. Everything else in the report is a
bin-convention artifact, and one stated "finding" is exactly that.**

---

## 1. What reproduces

`21+`, φ=0.280, t=3.94 on 2025 Bovada — **reproduces**. I get φ=0.2778, t=3.90 under my own
bin convention and φ=0.280, t=4.09 under the script's. The null control reproduces too:
P(home cover)=0.5243, P(over)=0.4956 on N=904, both within sampling noise of 0.50.

The joint table correctly restricted itself to `|spread| >= 14` and did **not** widen to 7-14
despite the report's interpretation inviting it. The build did not follow the prose.

The Odds API balance is unchanged at 8,976 — CFBD only, as claimed.

## 2. The defect: the bins changed between the probe and the OOS test

`joint_correlation_oos_2025.md` states: *"Buckets: 0-3, 3-7, 7-14, 14-21, 21+. SAME as the
probe. Not re-cut."*

The **labels** are the same. The **edges** are not.

- The discovery probe used `pd.cut(..., [-.01, 3, 7, 14, 21, 100])` — **right-closed**:
  `|spread| = 3` falls in `0-3`, `7` in `3-7`, `21` in `14-21`.
- `ncaaf/pipeline/test_joint_correlation.py:98` uses
  `(abs_spread >= lo) & (abs_spread < hi)` — **left-closed**: `3` falls in `3-7`, `7` in
  `7-14`, `21` in `21+`.

In football, 3 / 7 / 14 / 21 are the modal spreads. A boundary convention is not a cosmetic
choice here — it reallocates a large mass of games across exactly the boundaries being tested.
Both totals come to N=904; the per-bucket splits differ substantially (`0-3`: 115 vs 179;
`21+`: 198 vs 184).

This is the drift pre-registration exists to catch, and it was invisible precisely because the
labels matched. Nobody tuned anything. The convention was simply re-chosen when the analysis
was re-implemented, and the report then asserted sameness that was never checked.

boundary, not college football.

## 4. Check 3 — the research object is not the live object

N05 established `spread` = closing line, `spreadOpen` = open, differing on **85%** of rows.
Both the OOS test and `joint_outcome_table_v1.parquet` key on `spread`, the **close**. The
report notes in one line that live application must use `spreadOpen`, then builds on the close
anyway.

This matters unevenly, which is the useful part: `21+` is if anything *stronger* on the
opening line (t=4.20 vs 3.90 in 2025), while `14-21` collapses (t=1.28). Rebuilding the table
on the quantity actually available at bet time would likely reduce it to the `21+` rows.

## 5. Other observations

- **Cell sizes are small.** 8 cells, n = 100–175. The `21+ / OU<48` cell claims delta = +0.095
  on n=100.
- **The table pools discovery and validation** (2022–2025). Defensible for estimating magnitude
  once the hypothesis is confirmed, but the cell values themselves have no clean OOS estimate.
  The spec should say so.
- **The effect grew out of sample** (φ 0.196 → 0.280). Effects normally shrink. Given the
  robustness matrix this looks like sampling variation on N≈190 rather than a problem, but it
  is not a point in the result's favour and should not be reported as one.
- `NCAAF_BOARD_SPEC_v1.md` shows "+225 insertions" in `14d6cd323` only because the file went
  from untracked to tracked. The Cowork credit correction at §5 survived intact.
