WHY. build_ncaaf_tickets.py emits BOTH sides of every market as "legs": Georgia -24.5 and
Arkansas +24.5, Over 54.5 and Under 54.5. That is not a parlay and cannot be bet. Because
nothing selects a side, the AI was handed both and guessed — it has the favourite INVERTED on
2 of 5 tickets (Colorado is +3.5, the rationale calls it favoured; Nebraska is -5.5, the
rationale calls it "a -5.5 underdog").

WHAT CHANGES, STATED PLAINLY. Until now the AI could not affect what got bet. After this order
it chooses the sides, which makes it the selection mechanism and an unvalidated one. That is an
accepted trade for an entertainment build, not an oversight — but it must be written into the
spec, not left implicit. The N17 guard is UNCHANGED and still binding: the AI picks a side, it
never emits a number that enters pricing. No fair price, no probability, no projected total.

The inversion is an input defect, not a model defect. The AI received a list containing both
sides and no statement of who is favoured.

  1d. Write a test for 1b using tonight's actual ticket structure as the fixture — both sides
      of spreads and totals for one event. Show it RED against the current writer, then GREEN.

ITEM 2 — THE AI SELECTS

Extend the AI call so it returns, per game, a structured pick:

    {"legs": [{"market": "spreads"|"totals", "side": "<exact outcome_name from the board>",

     "abstain": true|false, "abstain_reason": "<string>"}

RULES, enforced in code after the response returns:
  - ABSTAIN IS ALWAYS ALLOWED and must be genuinely available — a picker that can never decline
    will pick noise on every game. Log abstentions and report the abstain rate.
  - At most ONE leg per market per game. Both sides is a raise (item 1b).
  - `side` must match a board outcome_name exactly; `point` must match that row (item 1c).
  - The N17 numeric-pricing guard STILL APPLIES to everything else in the response. A `reason`
    is prose. `fair_spread`, `projected_total`, `win_probability` and anything like them are
    still discarded and logged. Re-run the N17 test after this change and confirm it still
    passes — that guard is now protecting a model that has real influence.
  - The AI never chooses the BOOK or the PRICE. It picks market and side; the code takes the
    best number across the nine books. Selection and pricing stay separate.
