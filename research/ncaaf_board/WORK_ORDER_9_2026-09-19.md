ITEM 4 — THE GAP-TO-MARKET VIEW (BRANCH A ONLY)

This is the point of the whole exercise and it only exists once Hard Rock is captured.

With one bettable book, line shopping is meaningless — there is nothing to shop. What replaces
it is the GAP: where Hard Rock's number differs from the nine-book consensus.

  For every game, every market, emit:
    hardrock_point, hardrock_price
    market_consensus_point (median across the 9 books), consensus_no_vig_prob
    gap_points = hardrock_point - consensus_point
    gap_prob   = hardrock_no_vig_prob - consensus_no_vig_prob
    and the direction: which side the gap favours

  The board leads with this. It is the first thing in this build that is a REASON to take a
  side rather than a restatement of the line. On 2026-09-19 the AI justified Ohio State -52.5
  with "top-6 program at home vs weak FBS opponent" — a fact the spread already contains. A
  measured half-point gap against the market is a different kind of statement.

  DO NOT fit a threshold on what gap size is actionable. Report the gap; let the AI layer and
  Jeff read it. A fitted threshold would be a new signal and the standing football rule in
  CLAUDE.md forbids the search.

  NULL CONTROL: on a game where Hard Rock equals the consensus exactly, gap must be 0.0 and
  the board must show no direction. A gap metric that always finds a gap is measuring noise.

DECISION N27: gap-to-market as the board's lead column. Same commit.
