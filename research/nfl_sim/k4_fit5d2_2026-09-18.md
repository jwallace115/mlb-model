- K4 props are 2023-24 only. 2023 kickoff is unchanged (75).
- 2024 kickoff fix (75→70) affects drive starting position, but the

### Positive blind sides: NONE

No family has a positive blind side on fit_5d2. This matches fit_5d1
(post-D62 official actuals). The kneel diagnosis (D62) removed the only
positive cell.

### Directional prediction

The 2024 scoring rise cannot be measured from K4 alone (K4 measures
prop hit rates, not game-level totals). The prediction refers to the
team-level simulation, which is visible in the fit checkpoints but
not in the K4 props grading. The anchoring solver compensates for
any drive-length effect by adjusting EPA offsets, so the team-level
distribution stays centered on the market line. The prediction is
structurally correct but has no measurable K4 signal.
