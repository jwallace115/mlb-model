## Why this order exists

Every fix in this order is for a cause that was MEASURED first (5H + D98), not guessed:

1. After an offensive penalty the engine moves the ball back but never raises yards-to-go.
   Real football: yards-to-go rises by 6.75 on average, in 99.8% of 5,911 replayed downs,
   5.4 times a game. Sim: 0. Result: too few 3rd/4th-and-long (3rd-and-11+ 12.7% vs 18.2%),
   too many short 4th downs (+1.2/game), go rate +2.05pp like-for-like. 85% of that gap is
   state mix; the 4th-down TABLE is right (within-cell +0.27pp).
2. The clock table's late period starts at 2:00 of Q4. From 5:00 to 2:00 a tied or
   one-score-trailing offence runs on the `normal` cell — the same 38 s per completion as
   the first quarter. Real, Q4 121-300 s, tied/trail 1-8: pass 16.9 s, run 28.1 s.
   Sim: 25.4 s and 33.6 s. 94% of the sim's expiring tied drives never took a snap inside
   the 35: they ran out of clock getting there.
3. D89 (penalty rate denominator) — accepted in D93, verified on this Mac in D97.
